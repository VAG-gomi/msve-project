## Evidence classifications used in this bank

- **ORIGINAL-PRESERVED**: the original artifact is archived here and the
  copy was verified byte-identical against its source.
- **HASH-VERIFIED**: a measured digest matches a previously recorded
  expected value for the identified artifact.
- **REPORTED**: an earlier record (dated log, handoff, review) asserts an
  event or result; the underlying artifact was not re-examined here.
- **SUMMARY-ONLY**: the information survives only as a description; the
  original artifact was not found.
- **INFERRED**: derived from multiple records; not directly stated by a source.
- **UNVERIFIED**: not adequately checked.
- **UNAVAILABLE**: known or referenced but not currently accessible.
- **CONFLICTING**: sources disagree; discrepancy unresolved.

These labels describe *historical-evidence strength*. They do not certify
mathematical correctness of the design or validity of any decision.
